    Reactive brain on Lasna

      Safety Guard

      Funds and permissions

      Flowlet doesn't hold your money. It holds your rules. Know exactly what the automation can touch.

      • Pays only the payees and savings address you set
      • Moves money only on a callback from your reactive contract via Reactive's proxy
      • Standing orders stop at the daily limit (the guard trips instead of overspending)
      • Auto-save never takes your wallet below the emergency reserve
      • Cannot add rules, change payees or raise limits (owner only)
      • Never holds your tokens or your keys
      Recovery path
      1. Pause all: every rule stops; the reactive contract drops its CRON and deposit subscriptions.
      2. Revoke access: allowance to 0. Nothing can move, and the reactive contract pauses itself.
      3. Your tokens never left your wallet, so there is nothing to withdraw. Only the vault's small native gas balance is withdrawable, by you.

      On-chain data is public: your address, rule amounts, schedules, payees and every transfer. Flowlet stores no names, emails or notes, only addresses and numbers.
